🎯 CONTROLS - REMEMBER THESE!
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🧍 Body Controller — Gestures

  🙆🏻‍♀️ RAISE BOTH ARMS     →  JUMP (↑)
  🙅🏻‍♀️ arms Cross on chest →  ROLL (↓)
  🚶🏻‍♀️ LEAN LEFT           →  LEFT LANE (←)
  🏃🏻‍♀️‍➡️ LEAN RIGHT          →  RIGHT LANE (→)
  
  Press 'Q' to quit
  Press 'R' to recalibrate

🖐️ Hand Controller — Gestures

  ✋🏻 Open hand — 5 fingers              →  JUMP (↑)
  ✊🏻 Fist — 0 fingers                   →  ROLL (↓)
  👆🏻🫷🏻 Move hand to left side of frame  →  LEFT LANE (←)
  🫸🏻👆🏻 Move hand to right side of frame →  RIGHT LANE (→)

🎓 HOW IT WORKS (SIMPLE EXPLANATION)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Camera sees you
2. AI detects your body positions (33 points)
3. Code analyzes your pose
4. Determines gesture (jump/roll/left/right)
5. Sends arrow key to computer
6. Game receives the key press
7. Character moves in game!

NO GAME MODIFICATION - Just keyboard simulation!

🔗 WHAT CONNECTS IT TO THE GAME?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The connection is through KEYBOARD SIMULATION:

  Your Gesture → AI Detects → Code Decides → PyAutoGUI Sends Key
                                              ↓
                                         Operating System
                                              ↓
                                         Active Window (Game)
                                              ↓
                                         Game Responds!

It's like having an invisible friend pressing keys for you based on
your movements. The game has NO IDEA it's not you pressing the keys!
